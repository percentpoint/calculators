[PercentPoint](https://percentpoint.com/) is a free collection of **68 online calculators** for grades and GPA, percentages, pay and salary, business pricing, statistics and everyday math.

- Every result shows the formula and a worked example, so you can check the math yourself.
- No sign-up, no download, nothing to install. Works well on a phone.
- Free to use.

**Website: https://percentpoint.com/**

## How the formulas work

A few of the formulas used on the site, as shown on each calculator page:

**Score needed on a final exam** ([Final Grade Calculator](https://percentpoint.com/final-grade-calculator/))

```
Needed on final = (target − current grade × (1 − w)) ÷ w
w = final exam weight as a decimal (25% → 0.25)
```

Example: current grade 85%, final worth 20%, target 80% → (80 − 85 × 0.80) ÷ 0.20 = 60% on the final.

**Weighted GPA** ([Weighted GPA Calculator](https://percentpoint.com/weighted-gpa-calculator/))

```
Class points   = letter grade points + level bonus (no bonus on an F)
Quality points = class points × credits
Weighted GPA   = total quality points ÷ total credits
```

**Percent change** ([Percentage Increase Calculator](https://percentpoint.com/percentage-increase-calculator/))

```
percent change = (new − original) ÷ original × 100
new value      = original × (1 + percent ÷ 100)
```

The divisor is the original value, not the new one.

**Profit margin** ([Profit Margin Calculator](https://percentpoint.com/profit-margin-calculator/))

```
gross profit = revenue − cost of goods sold
margin       = profit ÷ revenue × 100
```

Example: sells for $40, costs $22 → gross profit $18, gross margin 18 ÷ 40 = 45%.
